The Vision

What is
Project MXSA?

Project MXSA is an ambitious open research initiative to build a fully functional bionic arm that doesn't just move — it feels. Using advanced AI and biomechanical engineering, MXSA bridges the gap between prosthetics and true sensory experience.

The arm integrates haptic feedback technology that translates touch data into neural signals, allowing the user to sense pressure, texture, and temperature — experiences previously impossible with conventional prosthetics.

This is not just a device. This is a statement about the future of human capability.

🤖

AI Neural Control

Trained ML models interpret muscle signals and translate intent to motion in under 20ms. No lag, no guessing — pure intent.

⚡

Real-time Adaptation

The system learns from the user continuously, improving grip precision and reaction time every single week.

🖐️

True Haptic Sensation

Piezoelectric sensors transmit tactile feedback, restoring the sensation of touch through neural stimulation pathways.

Core Technology

Built Different.
By Design.

01
🧠

AI Neural Interface

Electromyographic sensors read muscle activity and a custom neural network translates raw bioelectric signals into fine motor commands — 12 degrees of freedom, real-time processing at the edge.

02
🌡️

Haptic Sensation System

An array of piezoelectric tactile sensors covers the fingertips and palm, relaying pressure, vibration, and thermal data through transcutaneous nerve stimulation in real time.

03
🔄

Adaptive Learning

The onboard AI doesn't just process — it grows. Reinforcement learning algorithms continuously optimize grip patterns, force calibration, and movement accuracy for each user.

04
🦾

Biocompatible Build

Lightweight titanium chassis with medical-grade silicone contact surfaces. Designed for 16+ hours of daily wear with a fast-charge 72-hour battery system. Tough, sleek, human.

Common Questions

FAQ

What exactly does my money fund?
Your contribution funds hardware components (servo motors, EMG sensors, microcontrollers), AI model training compute, 3D printing materials, and development time. Every rupee is tracked and progress is documented publicly on this page and the GitHub repository.
Will the project be open-source?
Yes, 100%. All design files, schematics, AI model weights, and code will be released publicly once each milestone is hit. The goal is to make this technology accessible to researchers, makers, and anyone who needs it globally.
How can I track progress?
This website is updated with new photos, build logs, and milestone updates as the project advances. You can also follow the GitHub repository for code-level updates, circuit schematics, and full technical documentation.
Is UPI the only payment method?
Currently, UPI is the primary method for quick and fee-free transfers within India. If you'd like to contribute through another method, you can reach out via email or social media. International supporters are especially welcome — please get in touch.
